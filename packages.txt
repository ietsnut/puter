# Everything puter installs: Arch's packages, and puter's own from its
# pacman repository, [puter] (listed first, so its labwc, wlroots and qps
# are the patched ones), which CI builds and signs from packages/ and src/
# (packages/build). The apps are Qt Widgets ones, styled by puter's Qt
# style and stylesheet through qt6ct.
#
# puter runs on x86_64 (Arch Linux) and aarch64 (Arch Linux ARM). A package
# that exists on only one of them names it after its own name, and one for
# only one model of machine names that (its DMI product name) as well, and
# one for all but a model names it after a !; `puter-update packages` lists
# what this machine takes.
#
# This list is the whole of it: puter-update installs what it adds and
# removes what it drops, with whatever only that pulled in.
